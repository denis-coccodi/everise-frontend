# Party Finder

Every party being recruited in the game right now, on any data centre, without logging in to the game. Open it from **Community → Party Finder**. It starts on **Light and Chaos**, with all their worlds.

![Party Finder listings in Europe, Light and Chaos together](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/party-finder.png)

Recruiters' names and party descriptions are blurred in this picture.

## Finding a party

1. **Pick your region.** Since patch 7.58, the game shows the parties of every data centre in the same region together (Light and Chaos are one Party Finder), and so does the site. Each region says how many parties are up there right now, Europe first.
2. **Pick your world, if you like.** They're grouped by data centre. You then see what you can actually join from it: every data centre in your region, plus your own world's Hunt and FATE parties.
3. **Narrow it down:**
   - **Category:** every category is listed, even ones with nothing in them right now;
   - **Open slot for:** your role;
   - **Search:** words in the duty, description or recruiter;
   - **Beginners welcome only**, and listings without a duty (hidden unless you switch them on).
4. **Sort:** as in the game, or by time left, item level, players needed and more.
{: .steps}

## Reading a tile

Each tile is laid out like the game's:

- the duty and its icon, the recruiter and their world;
- the conditions and the description;
- the item level and the players still needed;
- the open slots as job and role icons. Hover an open slot to see which jobs it takes;
- the time left.

A sprout marks parties that welcome beginners. The list refreshes every 30 seconds while you watch, and keeps your place.

## Sharing a party (members)

- **Share as post:** puts the party in the feeds as a card, with your message. Tick the box to send it to the Discord too.
- **Share to Discord:** sends it to the free company's channel only, with a picture of its card.
- **When it ends:** you're told when the party is no longer up, and so is the Discord if it went there.

Every view has its own link, so you can send someone exactly what you're looking at.

> **Where the parties come from.** Square Enix doesn't publish the Party Finder. Players who run a community plugin report what they see, so a quiet hour or data centre may show fewer parties than the game does.
{: .note}

> **Light and Chaos.** From 10 November 2026 the game merges the Light and Chaos party finders. The site still shows them separately until it's clear how the merge works.
{: .note}
