# Getting started

## As a guest

You don't need an account to:

- read the home page's **Weekly** and **Hunts** tabs and the **Global Feed**;
- browse the **Party Finder**, **Events** and **Venues**;
- spin the **Duty Roulette** (Tataru, from the game, posts your result for you);
- try the **Fight Planner**, with a small hourly allowance, where it's switched on.

## Signing up

![The sign-in page](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/login.png)

1. Choose **Sign up** at the top right.
2. Pick how you'll sign in:
   - **Google or Discord:** one click, and the same button signs you in next time;
   - **an email address and a password:** 8 to 72 characters, with no required symbols, and not one from a known data leak.
3. With an email address, open the confirmation link the site emails you. It works for 24 hours, and the page has **Send the link again** if it doesn't arrive.
4. Optionally, add your character's name and world, so the site calls you by them, e.g. "Avar Kriss (Odin)".
{: .steps}

A quick check against bots may appear on the forms; it's usually invisible.

## What an account adds

- **Posting:** post, comment, favourite posts, and follow people. **Your Feed** shows the people you follow.
- **Sharing:** share posts, parties, events and venues to the free company's Discord.
- **Chatting:** talk with the characters in **The Waking Sands**.
- **Market History:** item prices across your region.
- **Notifications:** know when someone favourites or comments on your posts, or follows you.
- **Your settings everywhere:** your appearance (dark or light) follows you to every browser.
