# Market History

An item's price over time on every world of your region, a line per world, and where it sells cheapest right now. See where to buy before you travel. Open it from **Tools → Market History**. It's for members: an account keeps bots from using up the market lookups everyone shares.

![Market History](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/market-history.png)

## Using it

1. **Search for an item** by name.
2. **Pick your region.** Oceania's worlds can be added too, since any world can visit them.
3. **Choose:** high or normal quality, any dates you like, and which worlds to show.
4. **Read the chart:** each day's median sale price per world. Above it are the cheapest listing now, and the lowest prices today and over the days shown.
{: .steps}

- **Loading:** a long history fills in over a few seconds the first time.
- **Sharing:** the chart's link shows anyone the same chart.

> Prices come from **Universalis**, the community project that collects what players' uploads see on the market boards, back to 2022. Item names and icons come from XIVAPI.
{: .note}
