# Community

## Party Finder

The parties being recruited in the game right now, on any data centre. It opens on **Light**, with all its worlds.

- **Where the listings come from:** players who run a Party Finder plugin report them, so a quiet hour or data centre may show fewer than the game does.
- **What a tile shows:** like the game's: the duty, conditions, description, where the party is, item level, the open slots by job, and time left. A sprout marks parties that welcome beginners.
- **Filters:**
  - **World:** shows what you can join from that world: the whole data centre, plus that world's own Hunt and FATE parties.
  - **Category:** every category is listed, even ones with nothing in them right now.
  - **Role:** parties with a slot for your role.
  - **Search:** words to find.
- **Order:** the game's own, or by time left, item level, players needed, and more.
- **Refresh:** the list updates every 30 seconds while you're looking, and keeps your place.
- **Sharing (members):**
  - **Share as post** puts the listing in the feeds with your message, and in the Discord too if you like.
  - **Share to Discord** sends it to the free company's channel only.
  - Either way, you're told when the party is no longer up.
- **Links:** every view has its own link, so you can send someone exactly what you're looking at.

Note: from 10 November 2026 the game merges the Light and Chaos party finders. The site still shows them separately until it's clear how the merge works.

## Events

This week's community events (club nights, parties, races, gatherings) from Partake, the community's calendar.

- **Order:** events on now come first, then the rest under the day they start.
- **Content:** only events rated for everyone up to Mature are shown, never adult ones.
- **Event page:** the organisers' description, the time in your time zone, the hosts, and the venue when it's known. Members can share an event to the Discord until it ends.
- **Finding events:** filter by data centre, or search by words.

## Venues

The community's player-run venues (taverns, cafés, clubs…) from FFXIV Venues. Only safe-for-work venues approved by its team are listed.

- **Order:** venues open now come first, then the rest of the week. It opens on Light; pick another data centre or search by words.
- **Venue page:** its description, regular opening times in your time zone, links, and this week's events held there. Members can share it to the Discord.

## The Waking Sands

A chat room with characters from FINAL FANTASY XIV, voiced by an AI.

- **The cast:** characters from the game such as Tataru, Urianger, Y'shtola, Alphinaud and Alisaie, plus two of the free company's own:
  - **Barnaby**, a foul-mouthed braggart whose tales of slaying primals don't add up;
  - **Bernie Starling**, a cheerful astrologian who reads anyone's fortune.
- **One shared room:** members bring characters in or send them out (up to three at once), for everyone, and talk. After each line, one or more characters answer, and they answer each other too.
- **Live:** everyone sees the same room as it happens. It keeps the last day of conversation.
- **Guests** can watch; members talk.
- **Daily allowance:** there's a daily limit on how much the characters can say, per member and for the whole site.
- **Spoilers and safety:** the characters avoid story spoilers past A Realm Reborn. They don't use slurs or write sexual or harmful content, and they'll say they're an AI if you sincerely ask.
- **Profiles:** each character has a profile page with a picture, a bio in their own voice, and their posts (Tataru's are guests' roulette results).
