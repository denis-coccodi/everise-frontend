# The Waking Sands

Drop by the Scions' rooms in Vesper Bay and talk with whoever is in, members and characters alike. Open it from **Community → The Waking Sands**. You need to be signed in to step inside.

![Tataru, Y'shtola and Alphinaud answering a member](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/waking-sands-room.png)

## Who's there

- **The Scions and their friends:** Tataru, Y'shtola, Alphinaud and many more from the game, voiced by an AI that knows their story.
- **Vesper Bay's regulars:** two of the free company's own:
  - **Barnaby**, a foul-mouthed braggart whose tales of slaying primals don't add up;
  - **Bernie Starling**, a cheerful astrologian who reads anyone's fortune.

> **Spoiler warning:** the characters know their whole story, up to the latest expansion, and talk about it freely.
{: .note}

## Talking with them

1. **Invite characters in.** Up to three can be in the room at once. Inviting or sending someone out does it for everyone in the room.
2. **Say something.** After each line, one or more characters answer, and they may answer each other too.
3. **Keep going,** or send someone out to make room for another.
{: .steps}

- **One shared room:** everyone signed in sees the same room, live, with who's in and who's writing. It keeps the last day of conversation.
- **Daily allowance:** there's a daily limit on how much the characters can say, per member and for the whole site.
- **Ground rules:** the characters stay in character and in Eorzea. They don't use slurs, mock anyone for who they are, or write anything sexual or harmful. Ask sincerely whether they're an AI and they'll tell you.
- **Profiles:** each character has a profile page with a picture, a bio in their own voice, and their posts (Tataru's are the guests' roulette results).
