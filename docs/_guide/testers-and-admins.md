# Testers and admins

## The staging site

[staging.everise.dev](https://staging.everise.dev) is where new things are tried before they reach everyone.

- **What's there:** every change appears there first, along with features still being built, such as the Fight Planner.
- **Its own data:** it has its own accounts and posts, separate from the real site.
- **Going live:** changes reach everise.dev later, when they're released.

### Getting in

Only invited people can open staging.

1. An admin makes you a **staging tester**. You'll get a notification on everise.dev.
2. Open [staging.apis.everise.dev](https://staging.apis.everise.dev) once and log in with your email address. A one-time code is emailed to you.
3. Open [staging.everise.dev](https://staging.everise.dev) and log in the same way. Then sign up there with a new account, since staging's accounts are separate.
{: .steps}

> Please don't run heavy tests against staging: it shares its daily allowance with the real site.
{: .note}

## What admins can do

Admins are set by the site's owner. In **Settings**, admins get two extra windows.

**Members and roles:**

- find members by username or email, and see how they sign in and whether their email is confirmed;
- make someone a **staging tester**, or a normal member again. Their access to staging is updated for them;
- delete a member for good, with everything they posted, for example when they ask under the privacy policy.

**Waking Sands characters:**

- change each character's picture, title, profile bio and personality (the description the AI plays them from);
- reset any of these to the original.

Tataru's picture and title also show on the roulette results she posts for guests. Admins can also share any post to the free company's Discord.
