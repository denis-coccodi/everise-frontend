# Duty Roulette

Can't decide what to run? Three reels pick a duty type, a duty, and a way to run it, from the game's real duty list. Open it from **Tools → Duty Roulette**.

![The Duty Roulette](/docs/frontend/screenshots/roulette.png)

## Spinning

1. **Choose what's in the draw:** tick the duty types and roulettes, the PvP and Gold Saucer entries, and a level range. The menu link starts with Dungeons, Normal Trials and Normal Raids ticked.
2. **Spin.** The reels land on a duty type, a duty, and a way to run it: minimum item level, unsynced, everyone on one job, and more.
3. **Commence** posts the result to the feeds, like the game's "Duty Found" window.
{: .steps}

![A posted result](/docs/frontend/screenshots/duty-found.png)

- **Members** post it with their own comment, and can share it to the Discord.
- **Guests** get **Tataru** to post it for them, with a remark in her own voice.

Your setup is kept in the page's link, so you can bookmark it or share it. The **Idle animations** switch stops the reels and the crystal moving between spins.
