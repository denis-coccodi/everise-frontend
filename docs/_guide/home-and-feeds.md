# Home and feeds

The home page welcomes you with the free company's crest, and links to its Discord and its Lodestone page. Below are four tabs: **Weekly**, **Hunts**, **Global Feed** and, for members, **Your Feed**.

![The home page's Weekly tab](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/weekly.png)

## Weekly

- **Resets:** when the next daily, Grand Company and weekly resets come, counted down in your own time zone, with what each one resets.
- **Fashion Report:** this week's infographic (Kaiyoko's), with when judging opens.
- **Island Sanctuary:** this week's workshop recommendations (Overseas Casuals).

These are the posts the free company's Discord receives from the channels it follows, shown here as they appear in Discord.

## Hunts

The hunt trains the hunt groups announce, with a window per data centre (Light first):

- trains about to start ("Starts in 6 minutes");
- trains running now;
- the last one that started.

## The feeds

![The global feed](/docs/frontend/screenshots/feed.png)

- **Global Feed:** everyone's posts. **Your Feed:** posts from the people you follow.
- **Live:** new posts slide in at the top as soon as they're posted. You don't need to refresh.
- **Tags:** filter by topic. Popular tags sit beside the feed.
- **Cards:** some posts are cards, such as a Duty Roulette result ("Duty Found") or a shared Party Finder listing.

## Writing a post

1. Choose **New Post** on the home page.
2. Write it. Posts are Markdown, so you can use **bold**, lists and links.
3. Add up to 4 pictures, GIFs (search GIPHY right there) or YouTube videos. Large pictures are shrunk for you.
4. Tick **Also share in the Everise Discord** if the free company should see it there too.
5. Publish. You can edit or delete it later from its page.
{: .steps}

Comments can have one picture, GIF or video. Favouriting or commenting on a post lets its author know.

## Sharing to Discord

Nothing goes to Discord unless someone asks.

- **When you post:** tick **Also share in the Everise Discord**.
- **Afterwards:** use **Share to Discord** on the post. It's for the post's author, and admins can use it on any post. You can share once a minute.
