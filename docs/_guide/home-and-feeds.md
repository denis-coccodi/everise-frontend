# Home and feeds

The home page has four tabs: **Weekly**, **Hunts**, **Global Feed** and **Your Feed**.

## Weekly

- **Resets:** when the next daily, Grand Company and weekly resets come, in your own time zone.
- **Fashion Report:** this week's infographic (Kaiyoko's), with the days judging is open.
- **Island Sanctuary:** this week's workshop recommendations (Overseas Casuals).

These come from Discord channels the free company follows, shown as they appear there and updated every couple of minutes.

## Hunts

The hunt trains the hunt groups announce, with a window per data centre (Light first): trains about to start ("Starts in 6 minutes"), trains running now, and the last one that started.

## Global Feed and Your Feed

- **Global Feed:** everyone's posts. **Your Feed:** posts from the people you follow (members only).
- **Live:** new posts appear at the top as soon as they're posted.
- **Tags:** filter the feed by a topic.
- **Special posts:** some posts show as cards, such as Duty Roulette results ("Duty Found") and shared Party Finder listings.

## Posting

Members write posts with the **New Post** button on the home page, and edit their own from the post's page.

- **Media:** a post can have up to 4 pictures, GIFs or YouTube videos; a comment can have one. Large pictures are shrunk for you.
- **GIFs:** search for one from GIPHY.
- **Comments and favourites:** comment on posts and favourite the ones you like. The author gets a notification.

## Sharing to the free company's Discord

Nothing goes to Discord unless you ask.

- **New posts:** tick **Also share in the Everise Discord** when you post.
- **Posts already up:** use **Share to Discord**. It's for the post's author, and admins can use it on any post. You can share once a minute.
