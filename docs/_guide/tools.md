# Tools

## Duty Roulette

Can't decide what to run? Three reels pick a duty type, a duty, and a way to run it: minimum item level, unsynced, everyone on one job, and more. It all comes from the game's real duty list.

- **Choosing what's in the draw:** tick the duty types and roulettes, the PvP and Gold Saucer entries, and a level range. The menu link starts with Dungeons, Normal Trials and Normal Raids ticked.
- **Saving your setup:** your choices are kept in the page's link, so you can bookmark or share your setup.
- **Commence** posts the result to the feeds as a "Duty Found" card.
  - **Members** post it with their own comment, and can share it to the Discord.
  - **Guests** get **Tataru** to post it for them, with a remark in her own voice.
- **Animations:** the **Idle animations** switch stops the reels and the crystal moving between spins.

## Market History

One item's price over time on every world of a region, a line per world, so you can see where it sells for least.

- **Where the prices come from:** sales players report to Universalis, back to late 2022.
- **Above the chart:** the cheapest listing right now, and the lowest prices today and over the days shown.
- **Options:** pick the region, normal or high quality, the number of days, and the worlds. Oceania can be added from anywhere, since anyone can travel there.
- **Loading:** a long history fills in over a few seconds.
- **Sharing:** the chart's link shows anyone the same chart.
- **Members only:** guests see what the page does and a way to sign up.

## Fight Planner

Plans a party's heals and mitigations for a pull of an Extreme, Savage or Ultimate fight, from that party's FFLogs report.

- **How to use it:** paste the report's link (it must be public or unlisted on FFLogs) and pick a pull. The page shows each player's actions as played next to what the plan suggests.
- **Guests:** they can plan too, up to 10 new reports and 10 new pulls an hour.
- **Where it's available:** it's still being built, so it's switched on only on the staging site for now.
