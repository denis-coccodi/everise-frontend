# Events and Venues

Both are open to everyone, under **Community** in the menu.

## Events

Club nights, parties, races and gatherings players run in the game this week, from **Partake**, the community's event calendar.

![An event's card](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/events.png)

- **Order:** events on now come first, then the rest under the day they start, in your time zone.
- **Filtering:** pick a data centre, or search by name, host, world or what it is.
- **Content:** only events rated for everyone up to **Mature** are shown, never adult ones.
- **Event page:** the organisers' description, when and where it is, who hosts it, and the venue when the site is sure which one it is.
- **Sharing (members):** share an event to the Discord, until it ends.

## Venues

The community's player-run venues (taverns, cafés, clubs and more), from **FFXIV Venues**. Only safe-for-work venues its team approved are listed.

![Venues found by searching "cafe"](/docs/frontend/screenshots/guide/venues.png)

Banners, hosts and descriptions are blurred in these pictures.

- **Order:** venues open now come first, then the rest of the week. It starts on Light; pick another data centre, or search.
- **Venue page:** its full description, regular opening times in your time zone, its links, and this week's events held there.
- **Sharing (members):** share a venue to the Discord.

> These lists are as fresh as their sources: the site checks Partake and FFXIV Venues every few minutes while someone is looking.
