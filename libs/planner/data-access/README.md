# planner-data-access

The fight planner's data. `PlannerService`: `GET /api/planner/available`, `GET /api/planner/reports/:code` (an FFLogs report's players and its Extreme, Savage and Ultimate pulls), `GET /api/planner/reports/:code/pulls/:pullId` (everything that happened in one pull) and `GET /api/planner/catalog` (the jobs' actions with their heals and mitigations). The reports and pulls are for members.

- `PlannerStore`: the tool's state, provided by it: the report and pull in the query string (`?report=<code>&pull=<id>`), the report, the pull and the catalog as resources, the pulls grouped by fight, and `open()` for a pasted link.
- `PlanStore`: the plan for the pull open, provided by the tool: the HP margin, and running `@everise/planner/scheduler` in the browser, loaded with `injectAsync()` (prefetched when idle) so it stays out of the page's bundle; its `Plan` types are type-only imports.
- `report-link.ts` reads the report (and pull) a pasted FFLogs link or code names. `player-log.ts` turns a pull into one player's log: the hits they took and what covered each (their mitigations and shields, and the debuffs on the enemy that hit them), the heals and mitigations they used, their deaths. `effect-text.ts` says what an action's effects do in a few words; `pull-time.ts` gives times in a pull ("1:05"). `plan-log.ts` turns a plan into one player's part (what to use when, and each hit with the plan's mitigations and shields up, and what the shields absorb) and counts the hits it leaves short of the margin.
