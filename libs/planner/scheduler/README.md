# planner-scheduler

The fight planner's scheduler: plans a pull in the browser. Only ever loaded on demand (`injectAsync(() => import('@everise/planner/scheduler').then((m) => m.PlannerScheduler))`), so it stays out of the planner page's bundle.

- `mechanics.ts` groups a pull's hits into boss mechanics (raidwides, tank busters, targeted), leaving out ticks, auto-attacks and hits that did no damage. A hit more than three times what the attack usually does to a player (the median of its hits in the pull, by the attack's name, as the game gives some variants their own ids) and more than their max HP is a failed mechanic (an unsoaked tower, a stack taken alone): the plan takes it as avoided, spending nothing on it and counting it neither in their HP nor as short.
- `heal-power.ts` reads each player's heal power from the pull: HP healed per potency, from their direct heals (overheal included) that no healing buff was up for (a potency one on the caster, a received one on the target, as played), with their role's or the party's for those who didn't heal.
- `tools.ts` lists the mitigations and shields each player has at their level, from the action catalog (actions a trait replaces left out, statuses that don't stack across jobs counted as one), each shield sized from the caster's heal power or max HP, and the tanks' invulnerabilities (`INVULNERABLE`): Hallowed Ground (the hits do nothing), Holmgang (they land but can't take the last HP) and Superbolide (the tank's HP drops to 1 as it's used, then the hits do nothing) and Living Dead (the hits land but can't take the last HP, and the tank must be healed by their max HP in the 10s of Walking Dead after the first, or die: `walking-dead.ts` gives the heal plan a check then, that the tank is full, and a hit under it the heals can't bring them full after is short). What really limits some actions isn't their recast: gauges, cards and buffs are pools (`POOLS`), and actions another grants (`GRANTED`: Divine Caress once in the 30s after Temperance, Accession and Manifestation during Seraphism's 20s, Earth's Reply after Riddle of Earth) are planned only after the plan has the action that grants them (`fitsGrant`). Improvised Finish is a pool of one every 120s, Improvisation itself not being planned.
- `mitigation-plan.ts` plans them: the most dangerous mechanics first, each given mitigations and shields until every hit leaves the HP margin (so long cooldowns aren't spent early on small hits), respecting recasts, charges and shared recasts; GCD shields count for less than oGCDs, so they come last and are never spent on spare margin. The tanks' invulnerabilities go first where the options put them (`invulnerabilities`: a mechanic each, or never), by default where the pull had them (`invulnerabilities.ts`: the first mechanic that hit the tank while it was up), as that's the group's strategy: a mechanic one tank takes alone, or makes simpler for everyone. Those their recast doesn't allow come back in the plan's `unplaced`. Any other goes only on a tank's hit nothing else can make safe; the uses tried for that mechanic go back, for later ones. A hit under one counts as safe. Then what's left goes where it adds the most margin. `coverage.ts` says what a hit does with the planned uses up: mitigations first, each taking its share for the hit's damage type (physical or magic, from the timeline; none of unique damage; the smaller where the log doesn't say), then shields absorb what's left, each only for as many hits as it lasts.
- `heal-tools.ts` lists the heals the plan counts on: the healers' heals and healing over time, and the heals of the mitigations it plans, sized like the shields. Delayed heals land later than they're used: Earthly Star heals 720 potency once grown, on its own 20s after it's placed or set off with Stellar Detonation from 10s (the plan prefers going off on its own, and adds the detonation where there isn't the time). Triggered heals wait for their target: Excogitation at 50% HP or below, Catharsis of Corundum and Vigilant below 50%, each or when it ends, and Exaltation when it ends, read from the catalog's conditions. The heal pass puts one up before the hit that should set it off (the last mechanic's), and finds when it goes off from the HP track; its log names the heal by its status. Heals by stacks heal for each stack left when they end: each hit uses one up, on the barrier's target (Haima), on each member for their own (Panhaima), or on the white mage (Liturgy of the Bell, which heals the party each time). Macrocosmos heals when it ends, with half the damage each took meanwhile on top; Horoscope when it ends. `delayed-heals.ts` works out when and how much each heals.
- `hp-events.ts` and `hp-track.ts` follow each player's HP over the pull: the mechanics' hits as planned, every other hit (auto-attacks, damage over time, the floor) as played, and the healing the plan doesn't schedule (tanks' own, Kardia, the faerie, and the like) as played, with its overheal. A planned heal's healing as played is left out, by its action, its status, or the name the log gives it (Earthly Star's is Stellar Explosion).
- `heal-plan.ts` then plans the healers' heals in time order: before each mechanic, enough that every hit leaves the margin of the HP its player has then, respecting recasts, charges and gauges. Each heal's healing counts for what it costs, as the healers' guides rank them (`HealCost`, from `heal-tools.ts`): a free oGCD (Addersgall's included: spend it freely) first, then one spending Aetherflow (which Energy Drain would otherwise turn into damage), then a GCD that costs no damage (Afflatus Solace and Rapture, which Afflatus Misery refunds; Pneuma), then any other GCD. One whose gauge is full costs nothing, so lilies and Addersgall go before they'd overcap. The oGCDs that only pay with a GCD heal after them (`healPairings` in `heal-tools.ts`, `paired-heals.ts`) are planned with one, never on their own: Plenary Indulgence in a weave slot before a Medica, Medica III, Cure III or Afflatus Rapture, its Confession healing 200 potency more on each member the heal reaches (and its 10% mitigation then lowering the hits it reaches); Zoe before Pneuma or a Eukrasian heal, 50% more; Neutral Sect before an Aspected Benefic or Helios Conjunction, 20% more (its barriers aren't counted yet). After the heals the hits need, `spare-heals.ts` adds what a healer uses anyway: Assize and Celestial Opposition on cooldown, in the weave slots left; and, for a healer favouring damage (`favour`, per player; damage by default), the lily that would overcap, whenever the third one comes in (Afflatus Rapture, or Solace on themselves), so the Blood Lily charges Afflatus Misery, two kept for the heals. Lilies start empty at the pull and come one every 20s. A healer favouring healing spends no lily for damage, and their GCD heals count for more against the oGCDs. A heal that also mitigates (Kerachole, Holos, Temperance) lowers the hits it reaches, and the HP track gets back what they no longer take. A hit under an invulnerability needs no healing for; after Holmgang's hits or Superbolide the tank is at 1 HP, and the heals fill them up for the next. Each heal is multiplied by the healing buffs up for it (`healing-buffs.ts`): a spell by its caster's healing magic potency ones when it's cast (Temperance, Fey Illumination), any heal by its target's healing received ones when it lands (Rampart, Asylum, Mantra); one status counts once, different ones multiply. The buffs of actions the plan schedules count where it uses them, every other's (Neutral Sect, Krasis, Nature's Minne) as played. Each hit is then judged by the HP it leaves.
- `PlannerScheduler` (`@Service()`) puts them together: the mitigations as if every hit landed at full HP, then the heals. Where a hit is still short (no heal could fill the player up for it), it plans again from that plan: its mitigations stay (taking one away would leave the HP after it lower than planned for) and free ones are added for each hit at the HP the heals left it, then the heals again. Up to three rounds, as long as each leaves fewer hits short, or as many short by less. Only the players the options name (`replan`; everyone when left out) are planned: everyone else's mitigations, shields and invulnerabilities stay where they used them (`played-uses.ts`), and their heals in the pull's healing, as played; the plan fits around them.
- `weaving.ts` says when each player can act, from their GCDs as played (the rhythm of their rotation, their skill or spell speed in it). An oGCD takes 0.7s (its 0.5s lock and the round trip to the server) in the weave room between two GCDs: after the first's cast bar, or its 0.6s lock for an instant one, and before the next starts. So two fit after an instant GCD of 2.5s, one after a 1.5s cast, none after a 2s one. The oGCDs the player used that the plan doesn't schedule (damage oGCDs, raid buffs) keep their place. A planned oGCD goes to the latest free slot before its moment (or the first after it, for a heal as early as it can be), or the plan picks something else. A planned GCD (Adloquium, Medica III) takes the place of one of the player's GCDs, and its own cast bar sets the room after it. A player with no GCDs in the pull acts freely, an oGCD 0.7s and a GCD 2.5s from the next.

Not yet: Recitation (it needs critical heals), Krasis and Physis II put up for the heals after them, Kardia, Horoscope after a Helios, a delayed heal set off early (Horoscope read again, Microcosmos), shields made bigger by healing buffs, buffs only for the next spell (Zoe), the plan using a buff for its heals (Dissipation, Neutral Sect), a failure a player repeats every pull counted as one to heal through rather than avoided.
