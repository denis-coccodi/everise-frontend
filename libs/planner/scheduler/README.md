# planner-scheduler

The fight planner's scheduler: plans a pull in the browser. Only ever loaded on demand (`injectAsync(() => import('@everise/planner/scheduler').then((m) => m.PlannerScheduler))`), so it stays out of the planner page's bundle.

- `mechanics.ts` groups a pull's hits into boss mechanics (raidwides, tank busters, targeted), leaving out ticks, auto-attacks and hits that did no damage. A hit more than three times what the attack usually does to a player (the median of its hits in the pull, by the attack's name, as the game gives some variants their own ids) and more than their max HP is a failed mechanic (an unsoaked tower, a stack taken alone): the plan takes it as avoided, spending nothing on it and counting it neither in their HP nor as short. An attack whose every hit is unique damage leaving exactly 1 HP (TEA's Photon) takes them to 1 HP whatever their HP: a hit of their max HP that can't take the last HP.
- `heal-power.ts` reads each player's heal power from the pull: HP healed per potency, from their direct heals (overheal included) that no healing buff was up for (a potency one on the caster, a received one on the target, as played), with their role's or the party's for those who didn't heal.
- `tools.ts` lists the mitigations and shields each player has at their level, from the action catalog (actions a trait replaces left out, statuses that don't stack across jobs counted as one; of Galvanize and the Eukrasian barriers the one up first stays, as no other lands on a player who has one, whatever its size: `FIRST_STAYS`, from 63 ultimate kills' logs), each shield sized from the caster's heal power or max HP, and the tanks' invulnerabilities (`INVULNERABLE`): Hallowed Ground (the hits do nothing), Holmgang (they land but can't take the last HP) and Superbolide (half the tank's max HP comes off as it's used, since 7.1, then the hits do nothing) and Living Dead (the hits land but can't take the last HP, and the tank must be healed by their max HP in the 10s of Walking Dead after the first, or die; the hits keep landing that way until Walking Dead ends, past Living Dead's own 10s, as Undead Rebirth does in the game: `walkingDeadEnd` in `coverage.ts`). The Dark Knight mostly heals themselves out of it: `walking-dead.ts` gives each of their GCDs as played, while Living Dead and Walking Dead last, its 1,500 potency heal, and the heal plan a check when Walking Dead ends that the tank is full; the healers make up what's left (and don't heal for the hit that sets it off), and a hit under it they can't bring the tank full after is short. What really limits some actions isn't their recast: gauges, cards and buffs are pools (`POOLS`; an astrologian's card comes every 110s, Astral and Umbral Draw taking turns every 55), and actions another grants (`GRANTED`: Divine Caress once in the 30s after Temperance, Accession and Manifestation during Seraphism's 20s, Earth's Reply after Riddle of Earth) are planned only after the plan has the action that grants them (`fitsGrant`). Improvised Finish is a pool of one every 120s, Improvisation itself not being planned.
- `dark-knight-edges.ts` counts the Edges of Shadow more than played a replanned Dark Knight's MP pays for with the plan (`spareEdges`): the MP a Blackest Night that breaks gives back and what the plan saves go to damage, each Edge at the earliest moment that never runs their MP out later nor drops an Edge as played. `MpModel.plannedMp` gives them (`gained`) next to those it drops (`dropped`), and the Dark Knight's tab says both and the difference.
- `tank-autos.ts` puts each replanned tank's cooldowns, the short ones (The Blackest Night, Holy Sheltron or Sheltron, Heart of Corundum or Heart of Stone, Bloodwhetting or Raw Intuition, Oblation) and the bigger ones that work on physical hits (Rampart, Reprisal, Guardian or Sentinel, Bulwark, Damnation or Vengeance, Shadowed Vigil or Shadow Wall, Great Nebula or Nebula, Camouflage), on the boss's auto-attacks they take, each whenever it's ready, after each round's mitigation plan: its recast and gauge leave room for every use the mechanics have, and it needs two autos in its time, or, for The Blackest Night, to break (and the Dark Knight's MP as it goes up).
- `mitigation-plan.ts` plans them: the most dangerous mechanics first, each given mitigations and shields until every hit leaves the HP margin (so long cooldowns aren't spent early on small hits; a player's own one waits for their bigger hit still to plan, bigger by its size against their max HP, not by the HP an earlier round left them, so a tank keeps theirs for their tank busters), respecting recasts, charges and shared recasts; GCD shields count for less than oGCDs, so they come last and are never spent on spare margin; once every hit has what it needs, a GCD shield after which its healer goes below 30% MP within 30s, and that the cooldowns picked after it made unneeded, is taken back, the earliest of the healer lowest on MP first; each hit is judged with what the same player's hits in the 10s before took more without it (`mitigation-take-back.ts`). The tanks' invulnerabilities go first where the options put them (`invulnerabilities`: a mechanic each, or never), by default where the pull had them (`invulnerabilities.ts`: the first mechanic that hit the tank while it was up), as that's the group's strategy: a mechanic one tank takes alone, or makes simpler for everyone. Those their recast doesn't allow come back in the plan's `unplaced`. Any other goes only on a tank's hit nothing else can make safe; the uses tried for that mechanic go back, for later ones. A hit under one counts as safe. Then what's left goes where it adds the most margin. `coverage.ts` says what a hit does with the planned uses up (only those from the 2 minutes before it, `REACH_MS`: no effect lasts longer, and skipping the rest made the bench's 88 kills plan the same in 26% less time, the mitigation step projecting every hit for each candidate): mitigations first, each taking its share for the hit's damage type (physical or magic, from the timeline; none of unique damage; the smaller where the log doesn't say), then shields absorb what's left, each only for as many hits as it lasts; a GCD's shield (Adloquium's, the Eukrasian ones) grows with its caster's healing potency buffs when cast (Temperance, Fey Illumination, Neutral Sect), those the plan has up and the rest as played. A cooldown goes up as early as its longest mitigation still covers the hit (`reachMs`: Bloodwhetting's 8s, not Stem the Flow's 4s; a shield doesn't widen it), each moment counting for what's still up at the hit, so the latest, its short part covering the hit too, is taken whenever the tank can act then. A cooldown cast on someone else (Intervention, Nascent Flash) never goes on its user; Nascent Flash, sharing Bloodwhetting's recast, goes on a raidwide only where a hit is short of the margin (`mostEndangered` in `mitigation-moments.ts`).
- `heal-tools.ts` lists the heals the plan counts on: the healers' heals and healing over time, those of a DPS and a tank it replans (`role-heals.ts`: Second Wind, Curing Waltz, Equilibrium, Aurora...), and the heals of the mitigations it plans, sized like the shields. Delayed heals land later than they're used: Earthly Star heals 720 potency once grown, on its own 20s after it's placed or set off with Stellar Detonation from 10s (the plan prefers going off on its own, and adds the detonation where there isn't the time), and it can go down up to 15s before the pull, in the countdown, for the first hits. Triggered heals wait for their target: Excogitation at 50% HP or below, Catharsis of Corundum and Vigilant below 50%, each or when it ends, and Exaltation when it ends, read from the catalog's conditions. The heal pass puts one up before the hit that should set it off (the last mechanic's), and finds when it goes off from the HP track; its log names the heal by its status. Heals by stacks heal for each stack left when they end: each hit uses one up, on the barrier's target (Haima), on each member for their own (Panhaima), or on the white mage (Liturgy of the Bell, which heals the party each time). Macrocosmos heals when it ends, with half the damage each took meanwhile on top; Horoscope when it ends. `delayed-heals.ts` works out when and how much each heals.
- `hp-events.ts` and `hp-track.ts` follow each player's HP over the pull: the mechanics' hits as planned, the boss's auto-attacks with the plan's mitigations and shields rather than those up as played (`auto-damage.ts`: each from its damage before mitigation, a shield used up auto by auto; one the log has no such damage for stays as dealt), every other hit (damage over time, the floor) as played, and the healing the plan doesn't schedule (tanks' own, Kardia, the faerie, and the like) as played, with its overheal. A planned heal's healing as played is left out, by its action, its status, or the name the log gives it (Earthly Star's is Stellar Explosion).
- `heal-plan.ts` then plans the healers' heals in time order: before each mechanic, enough that every hit leaves the margin of the HP its player has then, respecting recasts, charges and gauges. Then (`refill`, as the planner does) everyone is refilled to full before each of the pull's mechanics with what those heals leave: a refill uses only what fits around every use planned, later ones too, MP lasting for every later heal; a GCD heal only from a healer with at least 30% MP, the fuller preferred (`heal-refill.ts`), and only before a heavy mechanic: one with a hit taking, mitigated but before shields, 40% of its target's max HP (50% when it lands on fewer than 4). Before the others only what costs no damage refills (oGCDs, the Afflatus heals, Pneuma; not an Aetherflow stack, which costs the Energy Drain it would have been, so Indomitability refills only before the heavy ones, and Lustrate never but with `everywhere`: kept for a hit that needs it, as the logs' scholars keep its stack for Energy Drain, 23 on a tank in 54 kills to the plan's 113, now 28), as the logs' healers leave players near 90% there and top them up only before the heavy ones (63 ultimate kills). Before every raidwide, light or heavy (on 4 players or more, `raidwide-shields.ts`; the logs' healers shield 60-67% of an ultimate's raidwides with a GCD shield whatever their size), everyone also goes in shielded, and GCD heals refill there too: there a hit no shield takes any of yet lacks its damage too, less what a candidate's barrier saves of it (`barrierFor`), so a GCD shield goes up even on a full party, and none on a player already shielded. Where that plan leaves a hit short or someone dead, `plan-variants.ts` plans again with shields only for the heavy raidwides (`heavyRaidwides` in `hit-damage.ts`; `noRaidwideShields: 'light'`), then with every heal refilling everywhere (`refillEverywhere`, the last-resort heals counting as any GCD heal) and keeps the one with fewer players dying (`deadIn`, to any damage: a damage-over-time tick too), then the `better` one; where that's still short, again without the heavy raidwides' shields (`noRaidwideShields`), then that way refilling everywhere, the better one kept each time. Each hit records the HP the needed heals alone give it (`hpNeeded`, from `judged-hits.ts`), which the next round's mitigations plan for. Each heal's healing counts for what it costs, as the healers' guides rank them (`HealCost`, from `heal-tools.ts`): a free oGCD (Addersgall's included: spend it freely) first, then one spending Aetherflow (which Energy Drain would otherwise turn into damage), then a GCD that costs no damage (Afflatus Solace and Rapture, which Afflatus Misery refunds; Pneuma), then any other GCD, and last the GCD heals The Balance keeps for when the healer is out of MP or every better heal is down (`fallback`: Cure II, Medica, Benefic II, Helios, Diagnosis, Prognosis), which refill only in that second plan (Physick never planned at all, `NEVER`: Adloquium and the Aetherflow heals do its job, and as a last resort the plan still cast it 37 times to the logs' 9 in 54 kills; Cure and Benefic not at all where the healer has Cure II and Benefic II, `OUTDONE`: they heal the same member less for the same GCD, and only won ties where either filled the deficit, the plan casting Cure 49 times on the bench against the logs' 7); but Helios cast while its caster's Helios Conjunction (or Aspected Helios) regen is up counts as any GCD heal (`FOLLOW_UPS` in `heal-candidates.ts`), as 89% of the logs' Helios casts were. One whose gauge is full costs nothing, so lilies and Addersgall go before they'd overcap. A replanned tank's own oGCD heals (Equilibrium, Aurora: `TANK_HEALS` in `role-heals.ts`, with the DPS's) are planned as free heals like a healer's, in place of those played; Thrill of Battle stays a healing buff as played. The healing buffs a healer puts on a tank (`tank-buffs.ts`, `TANK_BUFFS`; heal tools with no heal of their own, so no longer kept as played): an astrologian's the Arrow, before the heals are planned, with each card, on the tank taking the biggest hit within a minute, in a weave slot up to 8s before it, so the heals after count it (the logs' astrologians put 83 of 110 on a tank); a scholar's Protraction the same way whenever its recast allows, but in the last pass (`aimed`), after every heal, in the slots they leave, its boost not counted (placed first, it took the weave slots of a Dancing Mad scholar short of them: two deaths). On the bench, the Arrow 0 to 273 (logs 117), Protraction 0 to 81 (153), no deaths added. A replanned DPS's Second Wind comes after every healer's heal (`self`, from `heal-tools.ts`) and never refills: DPS press it to save themselves where a healer would be too late, not to heal for the healers. A GCD heal with a barrier (Succor, Concitation, Adloquium, Eukrasian Diagnosis and Prognosis) is weighed with what its barrier takes off the hits it reaches (`barrier-worth.ts`, from `projected` with everything planned so far, so nothing on a player whose barrier is already up); that weight only chooses: once picked, the barrier lowers the hits themselves. The oGCDs that only pay with a GCD heal after them (`healPairings` in `heal-tools.ts`, `paired-heals.ts`) are planned with one, never on their own: Plenary Indulgence in a weave slot before a Medica, Medica III, Cure III or Afflatus Rapture, its Confession healing 200 potency more on each member the heal reaches (and its 10% mitigation then lowering the hits it reaches); Zoe before Pneuma or a Eukrasian heal, 50% more, its barrier too (`shields` on the pairing; the use's `boost` sizes the shield that goes up), as the logs' sages follow 86% of their Zoes with Eukrasian Prognosis (the mitigation step, which plans those shields, weighs each with Zoe before it too, `zoeOptions` in `zoe-shields.ts`, Zoe a mitigation tool with no effects of its own: Zoes 74 to 279 on the bench, the logs 238; none on the `noRaidwideShields: 'light'` fallback); Krasis in the weave slot before a Sage's heal on one member (Druochole, Taurochole, Diagnosis, Eukrasian Diagnosis), 20% more on them; Physis II before any of a Sage's heals in its 15s, its own regen with it and 10% more, as the guides have it go first; Recitation before an Adloquium, Concitation, Indomitability or Excogitation, a critical heal (1.4 and a thousandth every 13.9 points of the healer's critical hit over 420, at level 100; 1.5 when the log doesn't say; its making them free of Aetherflow isn't counted); Neutral Sect before an Aspected Benefic or Helios Conjunction (Aspected Helios below level 96), 20% more, its barrier (250% of what an Aspected Benefic heals its target, 125% of a Helios Conjunction's on each member, for 30s) a shield for the hits after, weighed by what it saves there (`withBarriers` in `barrier-worth.ts`); one of those heals in a Neutral Sect already up (for Sun Sign, or an earlier heal) leaves the barrier too, nothing new pressed, and that barrier counts with the heal's other pairing as well (Horoscope before the same Helios: one Helios sets off both). A heal also counts what it makes up at the hits in the 30s after its mechanic (`AHEAD_MS` in `heal-candidates.ts`), at half weight, and a party heal in full (`PARTY_AHEAD_WORTH`): one heal on everyone that also covers the hits after is what healers cast instead of a heal on each member. A heal on one member refills only before a heavy mechanic (`refillsBefore`), and makes up only a tank's missing HP, or someone else's from a fifth of their max HP; once three or more such players are missing HP, only a tank's (`refillTargets` in `heal-refill.ts`), so party heals top up the rest. In 54 kills' logs, two thirds of the heals on one DPS went in with them missing 30% or more, 22 of 177 under 10%; refilling to full, the plan had put 416 of its 738 on a DPS missing under 10%. On the bench (88 kills, 25 of them Savage and Extreme) Essential Dignity on DPS went from 139 to 44 (logs 62), Tetragrammaton from 144 to 59 (43), with no deaths added. The single-target shields (`TANK_SHIELDS` in `tools.ts`: Celestial Intersection, Divine Benison, Taurochole, Haima, Aquaveil, Exaltation, the Spire) go on a tank (`onlyOn`, `mostEndangered` in `mitigation-moments.ts`), kept for the busters: on any other hit (a raidwide, or a hit on a few: FRU's Path of Light), or on someone not a tank, only where a hit is short of the margin (in 3 kills' logs, 1 of 45 on a DPS or healer before a heavy raidwide, the plan had 83; in 54, 45 on a tank for a hit on a few, the plan 656). The plan made the old way (`noRaidwideShields: 'light'`, `plan-variants.ts`' fallback) keeps them only from raidwides (`raidwidesOnly`), and the Spire on anyone, as before. The logs' astrologians put 93 of 129 Spires on a tank; the plan had 66 of 279, now 129 of 188. The raidwides in a replanned astrologian's Neutral Sect want everyone shielded with its barrier, as the heavy ones want any shield (`raidwide-shields.ts`). After the heals the hits need, `spare-heals.ts` adds what a healer uses anyway: Assize and Celestial Opposition on cooldown, in the weave slots left; in a last heal pass (`aimed`), an astrologian's Earthly Star and Macrocosmos and a sage's Pneuma whenever they're back (`aimedHeals`): Earthly Star at least 10s before the raidwide taking the party the most within a minute (grown by then), set off with Stellar Detonation in the first weave slot from 1s after it (`SET_OFF_MS`), or going off by itself at 20s where none is free (in 54 kills' logs, 216 of 302 detonations landed in the 10s after a raidwide, 67 before it), Macrocosmos and Pneuma in a GCD slot no heal took just before it (damage spells: on the bench Pneuma went from 65 to 196, the logs' sages casting it 139 times, safety the same), as the logs' players use them. `pull-planner.ts` plans every round without them (Rhizomata's and Lucid Dreaming's passes too), then, last of all, runs the picked plan's heal pass again (from the plan it started from, `sources`) with them on top of every other heal, and keeps that unless it comes out worse: more players dead at some point (`deadIn` in `plan-score.ts`, to any damage: a damage-over-time tick as well as a mechanic), or hits left short; placed in the rounds, their healing made the next rounds plan fewer shields (an FRU kill lost four DPS to Burnished Glory's bleed). On the bench (88 kills) Macrocosmos went from 45 to 70 (logs 92), Earthly Star 307 (405), safety unchanged and planning 26% quicker; for a healer favouring damage (`favour`, per player; damage by default), the lily that would overcap, whenever the third one comes in (Afflatus Rapture, or Solace on themselves), so the Blood Lily charges Afflatus Misery, two kept for the heals. Lilies start full at the pull (since 7.4) and come one every 20s. A healer favouring healing spends no lily for damage, and their GCD heals count for more against the oGCDs. A heal that also mitigates (Kerachole, Holos, Temperance) lowers the hits it reaches, and the HP track gets back what they no longer take. Attacks landing on a player at the very same moment (Dragonsong's Holy Orb and Dark Orb on both tanks, Ice Breath and Flame Breath) are judged together: the HP before that moment must last through all of them, each listed earlier taking its share first (`hit-damage.ts`). A hit under an invulnerability needs no healing for; after Holmgang's hits the tank is at 1 HP, after Superbolide at half their HP, and the heals fill them up for the next. Each heal is multiplied by the healing buffs up for it (`healing-buffs.ts`): a spell by its caster's healing magic potency ones when it's cast (Temperance, Fey Illumination), any heal by its target's healing received ones when it lands (Rampart, Asylum, Mantra); one status counts once, different ones multiply. The buffs of actions the plan schedules count where it uses them, every other's (Neutral Sect, Krasis, Nature's Minne) as played. Each hit is then judged by the HP it leaves. The heals for a mechanic go in from the last one before it there's room after; for a player's own damage outside the mechanics (`damage-checks.ts`: a tank's autos), from the last mechanic or their own check at least 10s before (`anchors`), so a stream of autos every 3s is healed for over its length, not in the moments between everyone's ticks.
- `PlannerScheduler` (`@Service()`) puts them together: the mitigations as if every hit landed at full HP, then the heals. Where a hit is still short (no heal could fill the player up for it), it plans again from that plan: its mitigations stay (taking one away would leave the HP after it lower than planned for) and free ones are added for each hit at the HP the heals left it, then the heals again. Up to three rounds, as long as each leaves fewer hits short, or as many short by less. Only the players the options name (`replan`; everyone when left out) are planned: everyone else's mitigations, shields and invulnerabilities stay where they used them (`played-uses.ts`), and their heals in the pull's healing, as played; the plan fits around them.
- `weaving.ts` says when each player can act, from their GCDs as played (the rhythm of their rotation, their skill or spell speed in it). An oGCD takes 0.7s (its 0.5s lock and the round trip to the server) in the weave room between two GCDs: after the first's cast bar, or its 0.6s lock for an instant one, and before the next starts. So two fit after an instant GCD of 2.5s, one after a 1.5s cast, none after a 2s one. The oGCDs the player used that the plan doesn't schedule (damage oGCDs, raid buffs) keep their place. A planned oGCD goes to the latest free slot before its moment (or the first after it, for a heal as early as it can be), or the plan picks something else. A planned GCD (Adloquium, Medica III) takes the place of one of the player's GCDs, and its own cast bar sets the room after it. A player with no GCDs in the pull acts freely, an oGCD 0.7s and a GCD 2.5s from the next. Finding a slot looks only at the player's actions within a minute of it (`weaving-near.ts`), longer than any cast bar or gap that could bear on it, not the whole pull.

Not yet: Recitation's sparing Aetherflow, critical heals otherwise (heal power leaves most of them out), Kardia, Horoscope after a Helios, a delayed heal set off early (Horoscope read again, Microcosmos), the plan using Dissipation for its heals, a failure a player repeats every pull counted as one to heal through rather than avoided.
