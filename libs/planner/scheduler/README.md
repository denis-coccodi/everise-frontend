# planner-scheduler

The fight planner's scheduler: plans a pull in the browser. Only ever loaded on demand (`injectAsync(() => import('@everise/planner/scheduler').then((m) => m.PlannerScheduler))`), so it stays out of the planner page's bundle.

- `mechanics.ts` groups a pull's hits into boss mechanics (raidwides, tank busters, targeted), leaving out ticks, auto-attacks and hits that did no damage.
- `heal-power.ts` reads each player's heal power from the pull: HP healed per potency, from their direct heals (overheal included), with their role's or the party's for those who didn't heal.
- `tools.ts` lists the mitigations and shields each player has at their level, from the action catalog (actions a trait replaces left out, statuses that don't stack across jobs counted as one), each shield sized from the caster's heal power or max HP. What really limits some actions isn't their recast: gauges, cards and buffs are pools (`POOLS`), and actions another grants (`GRANTED`: Divine Caress once in the 30s after Temperance, Accession and Manifestation during Seraphism's 20s, Earth's Reply after Riddle of Earth) are planned only after the plan has the action that grants them (`fitsGrant`). Improvised Finish is a pool of one every 120s, Improvisation itself not being planned.
- `mitigation-plan.ts` plans them: the most dangerous mechanics first, each given mitigations and shields until every hit leaves the HP margin (so long cooldowns aren't spent early on small hits), respecting recasts, charges and shared recasts; GCD shields count for less than oGCDs, so they come last and are never spent on spare margin. Then what's left goes where it adds the most margin. `coverage.ts` says what a hit does with the planned uses up: mitigations first, each taking its share for the hit's damage type (physical or magic, from the timeline; none of unique damage; the smaller where the log doesn't say), then shields absorb what's left, each only for as many hits as it lasts.
- `heal-tools.ts` lists the heals the plan counts on: the healers' heals and healing over time, and the heals of the mitigations it plans, sized like the shields. Delayed heals land later than they're used: Earthly Star heals 720 potency once grown, on its own 20s after it's placed or set off with Stellar Detonation from 10s (the plan prefers going off on its own, and adds the detonation where there isn't the time). Triggered heals wait for their target: Excogitation at 50% HP or below, Catharsis of Corundum and Vigilant below 50%, each or when it ends, and Exaltation when it ends, read from the catalog's conditions. The heal pass puts one up before the hit that should set it off (the last mechanic's), and finds when it goes off from the HP track; its log names the heal by its status. Heals by stacks heal for each stack left when they end: each hit uses one up, on the barrier's target (Haima), on each member for their own (Panhaima), or on the white mage (Liturgy of the Bell, which heals the party each time). Macrocosmos heals when it ends, with half the damage each took meanwhile on top; Horoscope when it ends. `delayed-heals.ts` works out when and how much each heals.
- `hp-events.ts` and `hp-track.ts` follow each player's HP over the pull: the mechanics' hits as planned, every other hit (auto-attacks, damage over time, the floor) as played, and the healing the plan doesn't schedule (tanks' own, Kardia, the faerie, and the like) as played, with its overheal. A planned heal's healing as played is left out, by its action, its status, or the name the log gives it (Earthly Star's is Stellar Explosion).
- `heal-plan.ts` then plans the healers' heals in time order: before each mechanic, enough that every hit leaves the margin of the HP its player has then, oGCDs first, respecting recasts, charges, gauges and one GCD at a time. A heal that also mitigates (Kerachole, Holos, Temperance) lowers the hits it reaches, and the HP track gets back what they no longer take. Each hit is then judged by the HP it leaves.
- `PlannerScheduler` (`@Service()`) puts them together: the mitigations as if every hit landed at full HP, then the heals. Where a hit is still short (no heal could fill the player up for it), it plans again from that plan: its mitigations stay (taking one away would leave the HP after it lower than planned for) and free ones are added for each hit at the HP the heals left it, then the heals again. Up to three rounds, as long as each leaves fewer hits short, or as many short by less.

Not yet: Kardia, Horoscope after a Helios, a delayed heal set off early (Horoscope read again, Microcosmos), heals that depend on healing buffs, tank invulnerabilities, avoidable hits (a failed mechanic, such as an unsoaked stack dealing millions, shows as a hit no plan can make safe).
