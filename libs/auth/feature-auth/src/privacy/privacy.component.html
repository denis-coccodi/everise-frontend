Privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Everise is a small, non-commercial fan site for Final Fantasy XIV players. This page says what it stores about you, why, and how to have it removed. Everise shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

What Everise stores

  • Your account: your email address, username, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password if you set one, and anything you add in Settings: a bio, a profile picture and your colour mode.
  • Signing in with Google, Facebook, Microsoft or Discord: if you continue with one of them, Everise receives your name, your confirmed email address and that account's id. It keeps the email and the id, to recognise you next time, and uses the name only to suggest your username. It never sees your password there and never posts anything for you.
  • What you post: your posts, comments, favourites, the people you follow, and roulette results you share. These are public, as on any feed.
  • Guests sharing a roulette result: a one-way fingerprint (hash) of the device's IP address, to stop spam. The address itself is not kept.
  • Admins: can see members' usernames and email addresses, to give out roles.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • token: keeps you signed in, for up to a day.
  • social_login: lives for a few minutes while you sign in with Google, Facebook, Microsoft or Discord, for safety.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your colour mode and roulette settings.

There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Where it is kept

Everise runs on Cloudflare, which stores the data and handles every request (and so sees IP addresses, as any web host does). Signing in with Google, Facebook, Microsoft or Discord also follows their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything stored about you, email {{ contact }} from the address of your account, with your username. It is removed within 30 days, and you get a reply when it's done. Posts and comments you made are removed with it.

If you signed in with another service, you can also remove Everise there, then email as above to remove the data Everise already has: Google (Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps), Facebook (Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites), Microsoft (account.microsoft.com → Privacy → Apps and services) or Discord (User Settings → Authorised Apps).

Questions

Write to {{ contact }}. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
