# market-feature-market

The Market History page (`/market-history`, `MarketComponent`, for signed-in members): one item's price over time on every world of a region, a line per world, drawn with `<cdt-line-chart>` from the UI library (lines break on days without sales). Above the chart, the lowest middle price today and in the days shown, with its world. `cdt-market-item-picker` searches items by name; `cdt-market-controls` picks the region (and Oceania), the sales (all, NQ, HQ), the days (dates or a quick range, a month by default) and a logarithmic price axis; `cdt-market-worlds` picks the worlds drawn, by data centre. The state is `MarketStore` and `MarketSearchStore` from `@everise/market/data-access`. `market-page-testing.ts` holds the specs' fixtures and the page as the app routes it.
